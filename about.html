WHO WE ARE

Beauty begins with intention.

Instaflora is a floral and gifting brand built around one simple idea: every meaningful moment deserves beautiful details.

From a single bouquet sent with love to an entire wedding transformed with flowers, we carefully design every experience with creativity, elegance and care.

Our collection brings together fresh flowers, luxurious bouquets, thoughtful gifts, hampers, chocolates, cakes and event décor — all created to help you express what words sometimes cannot.

OUR STORY

Born from a love for beautiful moments.

Instaflora began with a passion for flowers and a desire to make celebrations feel more personal, meaningful and unforgettable.

What started as a love for carefully arranged flowers grew into a complete gifting and event styling experience.

Today, we continue to create every arrangement with the same attention to detail that inspired us from the beginning.
