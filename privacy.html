We respect your privacy.

At Instaflora, we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you use our website, how we use that information, and the choices you have regarding your personal information.

By accessing or using the Instaflora website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

When you use Instaflora, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery details and other information needed to process your requests or orders.

We may also collect basic technical information such as browser type, device information, pages visited and general website usage information. This helps us understand how visitors use our website and improve the experience.

02

How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to provide and improve our products and services, process orders, respond to enquiries, communicate with customers and provide requested support.

  • Process and manage orders or requests.
  • Respond to customer enquiries.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Provide relevant updates and communications.
  • Help maintain website security and functionality.
03

Information Sharing

Instaflora does not sell your personal information. We may share necessary information with trusted service providers when required to operate our website, process orders, deliver products or provide services requested by you.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the rights, safety or security of Instaflora, our customers or others.

04

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, understand website activity and improve functionality.

Cookies are small files stored on your device by your browser. You may adjust your browser settings to manage or disable cookies. Some website features may not work correctly if cookies are disabled.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, while we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to request access, correction or deletion of certain information.

Our website is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. We encourage parents or guardians to supervise children's online activities.
